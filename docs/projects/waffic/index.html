Overview

Waffic (weather + traffic) is the web application I built for my bachelor’s thesis in Economic Informatics at the Romanian-American University. Before a drive, a user picks a destination and sees the driving route on a map, with the current weather at the start and at the destination. Every search is kept as a travel history.

It also has a business side. Companies can buy a collaboration plan that puts their location on the map and alerts drivers who pass nearby. Administrators answer contact messages and follow usage through statistics and reports.

The idea

Bring traffic and weather information into one interface, so drivers can avoid delays caused by not knowing the weather at their destination.

  • The weather where you start says little about the weather where you arrive, hours later.
  • Checking a navigation app and a weather app in turn, during the trip, is slow and distracting.
  • Local businesses along a route had no simple way to reach drivers passing by.

Who uses it

Visitor

Anyone who opens the site.

  • See the collaboration plans
  • Read the tutorial
  • Send a contact message
  • Create an account

Driver

A signed-in user.

  • Plan a trip with route and weather
  • Keep, star and delete past trips
  • Edit personal details
  • Buy a collaboration plan for a business

Administrator

A user marked as admin in the database.

  • Answer and archive contact messages
  • Follow statistics and reports
  • Search users, collaborations and destinations

What it does

Route and weather on one screen

Type a destination in the top bar. The map draws the driving route from where you are, and the top bar shows the weather at both ends.

  • Destination searchGoogle Places autocomplete in the top bar.
  • Driving route from your locationThe browser’s location is the start; Google Directions draws the route.
  • Weather at both endsCurrent conditions from AccuWeather, with an icon and temperature for the start and the destination.
  • Kept up to dateRoute and weather refresh every 10 minutes while the map is open.

Travel history

Every trip searched is saved, so frequent routes are one tap away.

  • Saved automaticallyStart city, destination and date for each search.
  • FavouritesStar the trips you repeat; delete the ones you do not need.

Collaborations for businesses

Companies buy a plan and appear on every driver’s map.

  • Five plansFrom 7 days for 5 RON to a year for 75 RON. A new plan replaces the active one, and the page says so before you buy.
  • Company pinsEach active company is a marker on the map, with its name on click.
  • Nearby alertWhen a driver is within about 500 m of a company, Waffic shows its name and description.

Administration

A separate menu section for administrators, visible only to them.

  • MessagesContact messages in a table; mark as answered or delete.
  • Statistics and reportsUsers with and without collaborations or trips, age distribution, top localities, plans and destinations, drawn with Google Charts.
  • SearchFind users by name, collaborations by date range, and how often each destination was searched.

Accounts and consent

Email and password accounts, with the essentials around them.

  • Sign-up and password resetFirebase Authentication, with a reset email for forgotten passwords.
  • GDPR consentA consent dialog after sign-in until the user accepts.
  • TutorialA help page that explains every screen, in Romanian like the rest of the interface.
