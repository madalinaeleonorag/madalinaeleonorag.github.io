Overview

UniversityRating, published as UniView, is the web application I built for my master’s thesis in Economic Informatics at the Romanian-American University. Future students search universities, faculties and bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral programmes, and see each one’s courses, facilities, location and reviews.

Content comes from two sides. Universities manage their own pages through representative accounts that an administrator verifies. Students leave star ratings and comments, and every comment goes through sentiment analysis in Azure Text Analytics: neutral and positive comments are published at once, negative ones wait for an administrator.

The idea

Bring information about universities into one place, kept up to date by a representative of each institution, with reviews filtered so they do not mislead future students.

  • Future students had to search each university’s website without guidance, and those websites gave no authorised person a way to keep information current.
  • Opinions about universities lived on social networks and forums, with no control, which created confusion.
  • Of the three similar platforms compared (UniApply, Optiuni.ro and Study in Romania), none let students write reviews or give ratings.

Who uses it

Student

Anyone looking for a university; an account is needed to review.

  • Search and filter at every study level
  • Save favourites
  • Rate and comment on universities and faculties

University representative

A student account upgraded after an administrator approves a request.

  • Ask to add or claim a university
  • Edit the university page in place
  • Add faculties, programmes and courses

Administrator

Keeps the content trustworthy.

  • Approve or decline representative requests, with a reason
  • Approve or decline flagged reviews
  • Follow statistics and reports

What it does

Search and compare

One search for every level of study, with the filters a future student actually uses.

  • Five levelsUniversities, faculties, and bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral programmes.
  • FiltersName, description, location, facilities and public or private, sorted by rating.
  • FavouritesA heart on every card, kept per level on the student’s profile.
  • University pagesMission, vision, values, strategic programmes, 15 kinds of facilities, contact details and a map.

Faculties, programmes and courses

The detail a student needs to choose a programme, not only a university.

  • Programmes by levelBachelor’s, master’s and doctoral tabs on each faculty page.
  • Programme detailsYears, semesters, general skills, career paths and the list of courses.
  • Course detailsCredits, evaluation, hours, semester and objectives.

Reviews with AI moderation

Students rate and comment; a sentiment check decides what goes live at once.

  • Stars and commentsFor universities and faculties.
  • Sentiment checkEach comment is analysed by Azure Text Analytics before it is saved.
  • Negative comments waitThey are held for an administrator, and the author sees a note that the review is being checked.
  • Declined reviews do not countThey are hidden and left out of the rating.

Verified university accounts

Universities keep their own pages up to date, after an administrator checks who is asking.

  • RequestName, type, description, contact, location, facilities, logo and photos.
  • ApprovalThe administrator approves, which creates the university page and links it to the account, or declines with a reason the user sees.
  • Edit in placeRepresentatives edit the same pages students see, section by section.

Administration and reports

An overview of users and content, and queues for what needs a decision.

  • StatisticsAbout twenty charts on users, institutions, programmes, facilities and requests.
  • ReportsTables for universities, faculties, programmes and users.
  • QueuesRepresentative requests and flagged reviews, each split into pending, approved and declined.

Others

  • Romanian and EnglishInterface text through translation files.
  • Legal pages and consentTerms, privacy policy, copyright and GDPR consent on the profile.
  • DirectionsA route on the map from the student to the university.
