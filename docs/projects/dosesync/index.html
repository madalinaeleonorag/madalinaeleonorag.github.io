Your medication plan, the way your doctor actually wrote it.

Most reminder apps assume one pill, every day, at the same time. DoseSync handles the rest: pills only on certain days of the month, a supplement only in winter, a 10-day course that follows your cycle, or a medicine you take only when you need it. It reminds you on time, keeps count of what is left, and keeps a clear record for your doctor.

What it does

Your day at a glance

Open the app and see what to take now. One tap records it; skipping asks why, so you and your doctor know.

  • AvailableToday at a glanceDoses grouped by morning, lunch, evening and night. The next one is always at the top.
  • AvailableTaken, or skipped with a reasonRecord a dose in one tap, or say why you skipped it. Undo is always there for five seconds.
  • AvailableTaken earlier…Forgot to tap? Record the dose at the time you actually took it.
  • AvailableReminders that work from the notificationTaken, Snooze and Skip right from the reminder, with names hidden on the lock screen if you like.

Schedules that fit real treatment plans

Most apps assume one pill a day. DoseSync follows the plan your doctor actually wrote.

  • AvailableFlexible schedulesEvery day, chosen weekdays, days of the month, every few hours, seasonal, once, or only when needed.
  • AvailableCourses that follow your cycleMark day 1 of your period and a 10-day course starts from it. The next start is estimated, never assumed.
  • AvailablePause without losing historyStop a treatment for a while and resume it later. Past records stay as they were.
  • AvailableSafe as-needed dosesA daily maximum and a minimum gap, with a clear warning before you log one too many.

Stock and refills

Know what is in the drawer before it runs out.

  • AvailableStock that counts itselfEvery dose you take is subtracted, and undo puts it back.
  • AvailableRuns out around…An estimated date for each medication, including courses that follow your cycle.
  • AvailableLow-stock reminderA notification when a count reaches the level you chose.

Trips and appointments

Life does not stop for a treatment plan, so DoseSync plans around it.

  • AvailablePlan a tripHow much of each medication to pack, and whether you have enough. Tick “flying” and the medication you take for flights is packed too.
  • AvailableAppointmentsDoctor visits and tests on Today and in the calendar, with reminders the evening before and two hours before.
  • AvailableTime zonesAbroad, keep taking doses at home time or move them to local time.

Is DoseSync right for you?

A good fit if

  • You take several medicines or supplements on different schedules.
  • Some of them follow the calendar: days 1 to 15 of the month, or October to March.
  • Some treatments follow your menstrual cycle.
  • You take some medicines only when needed and want a safe daily limit.
  • You want your health data to stay on your phone, with no account.
  • You are tired of subscriptions for basic features.

Not for you yet if

  • You need an iPhone app (Android comes first).
  • You want to track symptoms, mood or sleep. DoseSync only records what you took.
  • You need a family member to see your doses remotely (planned for later).

Your data stays yours

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Your data stays on your phone, encrypted. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • An optional PIN or fingerprint keeps others out of the app.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • Back up and restore your data yourself, whenever you want.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Core features will stay free, with no limit on the number of medicines.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start adding your medicines.

Will it tell me what dose to take?

No. DoseSync follows the plan you enter from your prescription. It does not give medical advice or change your treatment.

What if I change phones?

Export your data to a file in Settings and restore it on the new phone. DoseSync is left out of Android backups on purpose, so your health data never goes to the cloud.

What if I forget my PIN?

Open DoseSync with the PIN, pattern or password you use to unlock your phone, then choose a new PIN. On a phone without a screen lock, you answer which of your medicines you take.
